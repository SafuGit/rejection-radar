## What Rejection-Radar Is

Rejection-Radar helps you understand why your job applications might not be getting responses. When you apply for jobs and hear nothing back, or receive generic rejection emails with no explanation, it's hard to know what to improve. 

Rejection-Radar analyzes your CV alongside the job descriptions you've applied to, looking for patterns and mismatches that might explain the silence. We simulate how applicant tracking systems evaluate candidates, identifying keyword gaps, skill mismatches, and experience level signals that commonly lead to early-stage rejections.

This is not a magic solution. It's a tool that surfaces probable reasons based on what employers typically look for, helping you make more informed decisions about how to position yourself.

## Why This Exists

Applying for jobs has become a black box. You send your CV into company portals and recruitment systems, and most of the time, you get nothing back. No feedback. No explanation. Just silence or an automated rejection.

This lack of transparency makes it nearly impossible to learn and improve. You don't know if you're being rejected because of a missing skill, the wrong phrasing, too much experience, too little experience, or something else entirely. Without feedback, every application feels like a shot in the dark.

Rejection-Radar exists to give you some of that missing context. While we can't tell you exactly what happened inside a company's hiring process, we can help you see what might be working against you based on common hiring patterns.

## What Rejection-Radar Does

Rejection-Radar compares your CV against the job descriptions you've applied to and identifies:

### Skill Relevance and Gaps
Which required or preferred skills appear in the job posting but not on your CV, and how central those skills are to the role.

### Language and Keyword Mismatches
Whether you're using different terminology than the employer for the same concepts, which can cause your application to be overlooked by applicant tracking systems.

### Experience Level Signals
If the role expects a certain level of seniority or background that doesn't clearly match what's presented in your CV.

### ATS Simulation
We simulate how applicant tracking systems screen candidates, showing you what automated filters might catch before a human ever sees your application. This helps you understand what systems look for, even though we don't have access to actual company systems.

### Repeated Patterns Across Applications
If you're consistently applying to roles that ask for something you don't prominently show, Rejection-Radar highlights that trend so you can decide whether to adjust your approach or target different roles.

### Improvement Signals
Concrete, specific observations about what might strengthen your position, such as highlighting certain projects differently, adding measurable results, or clarifying your expertise in particular areas.

**Important**: Rejection-Radar does not rewrite your CV for you. It shows you what it sees, and you decide what to do with that information.

## What It Does NOT Do

Rejection-Radar has clear limits, and we're upfront about them:

### It Does Not Access Company Systems
We have no insight into actual applicant tracking systems, internal notes, or hiring decisions. We simulate common ATS behavior based on industry patterns, but everything we analyze is based solely on the documents you provide.

### It Does Not Guarantee Jobs
Following our suggestions does not ensure you will get hired. Hiring involves many factors we can't see or control, including company culture, internal candidates, budget changes, and subjective preferences.

### It Does Not Make Decisions for You
We provide information and observations. You decide how to interpret them and whether to act on them.

### It Does Not Train on Your Data
Your CV and application history are yours. We do not use them to train models or improve our service without explicit consent.

### It Is Not Absolute Truth
Our analysis is probabilistic. We identify likely issues, not certain ones. Hiring is human and unpredictable.

## How to Use It

Using Rejection-Radar is straightforward:

1. **Upload your CV** — Provide the CV you've been using for applications.

2. **Add job descriptions** — For each role you applied to, paste or upload the job description.

3. **Record the outcome** — Mark whether you were rejected, never heard back, or moved forward.

4. **Review your analysis** — Rejection-Radar will show you what patterns it identifies, what might be missing, and where mismatches appear.

5. **Decide what to adjust** — Based on what you learn, you can choose to update how you present certain skills, target different types of roles, or emphasize different aspects of your background.

Over time, as you add more applications, Rejection-Radar can show you broader trends in where you're gaining or losing traction.

## Privacy and Ethics

Your data belongs to you. Rejection-Radar is designed with privacy as a core principle:

- You control what you upload and can delete it at any time.

- We do not share your CV or application data with employers or third parties.

- We do not use your personal information to train our analysis systems without your explicit permission.

- We store only what's necessary to provide you with insights, and we're transparent about what that includes.

## Who This Is For

Rejection-Radar is designed for:

- Job seekers who apply regularly but struggle to understand why they're not getting responses.

- Professionals in competitive or technical fields where small mismatches can eliminate candidates early.

- Early and mid-career professionals who want to improve their positioning but don't have access to professional career coaching.

## Who This Is NOT For

Rejection-Radar may not be useful if:

- You're applying to very few jobs where outcomes are highly individual.

- You're in a field where CVs are less standardized or less important than portfolios or networks.

- You're looking for someone to rewrite your materials for you rather than provide analysis.

## Final Note

Rejection-Radar is a decision-support tool. It gives you context and patterns that are otherwise invisible. It does not replace your judgment, and it cannot predict or control hiring outcomes.

Job searching is hard, and the lack of feedback makes it harder. Our goal is to give you a clearer picture of what might be happening so you can make better choices about where to focus your effort and how to present yourself.

We can't promise you'll get the job. But we can help you see what you might be up against.
